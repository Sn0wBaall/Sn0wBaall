## Featured projects

### 🔎 [Packet-Scope](https://github.com/Sn0wBaall/Packet-Scope)
Analyze `.pcap` and `.pcapng` captures right in your browser: explore packets and conversations, spot potential anomalies, and export reports. **Captures stay on your device**—nothing gets uploaded to a server. `React` · `JavaScript` · `Networking`

### 🧪 [WarLabs](https://github.com/Sn0wBaall/WarLabs)
A collaborative, self-hosted platform for practicing pentesting in isolated Docker labs. It offers a GUI and CLI, with challenges covering enumeration, exploitation, and privilege escalation. `Python` · `Docker` · `Pentesting`

### 🖥️ [RevShell-kit](https://github.com/Sn0wBaall/RevShell-kit)
A terminal toolkit for generating reverse shell payloads in multiple formats, with a built-in listener. For authorized security testing only. `Python` · `CLI` · `Security`
